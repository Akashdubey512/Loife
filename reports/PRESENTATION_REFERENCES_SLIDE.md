## Slide Title: References & Scientific Baselines

### 1. Sustainability & Life Cycle Assessment (LCA)
* **Poore, J., & Nemecek, T. (2018).** *Reducing food’s environmental impacts through producers and consumers.* **Science**, 360(6392), 987–992.  
  *(Baseline LCA emission factors for CO2e, land use, and freshwater consumption across 42 food categories).*
* **FAO (UN Food and Agriculture Organization). (2019).** *The State of Food and Agriculture 2019: Moving forward on food loss and waste reduction.* Rome: FAO.  
  *(Global baseline data on 30–40% food loss and 8–10% global greenhouse gas emissions).*

### 2. Open Benchmark Datasets
* **Mendeley E-Nose Meat Quality Dataset (2020).** *Electronic Nose Dataset for Meat Spoilage Detection.* Mendeley Data, V1, doi:10.17632/n8mc3nspfn.1.  
  *(20,815 gas sensor time-series measurements across 8 MOS sensors).*
* **AI4I 2020 Predictive Maintenance Dataset.** *UCI Machine Learning Repository.*  
  *(10,000 machine failure records with physical process telemetry).*
* **Candanedo, L. M., et al. (2017).** *Data driven prediction models of energy use of appliances in a low-energy house.* **Energy and Buildings**, 140, 81–97.  
  *(UCI Dataset: 19,735 environmental microclimate measurements).*
* **Genpact Analytics (2018).** *Food Demand Forecasting Benchmark Dataset.*  
  *(50,000 fulfillment center order records across 51 meal categories).*

### 3. Operations Research & Vehicle Routing Algorithms
* **Clarke, G., & Wright, J. W. (1964).** *Scheduling of vehicles from a central depot to a number of delivery points.* **Operations Research**, 12(4), 568–581.  
  *(Clarke-Wright Savings algorithm for Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problems).*
* **Augerat, P., et al. (1995).** *CVRPLIB: Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem Library.* Pontifical Catholic University of Rio de Janeiro (PUC-Rio).  
  *(Standard CVRPTW benchmark instances).*

### 4. Machine Learning Frameworks & Security Standards
* **Ke, G., et al. (2017).** *LightGBM: A highly efficient gradient boosting decision tree.* **NeurIPS 30**, 3146–3154.
* **NIST Special Publication 800-132.** *Recommendation for Password-Based Key Derivation (PBKDF2 HMAC-SHA256).* National Institute of Standards and Technology.
* **RFC 7519.** *JSON Web Token (JWT) Architecture.* Internet Engineering Task Force (IETF).
